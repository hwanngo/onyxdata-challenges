## G2 integrity conclusions

Every count below is reproduced by `analysis/integrity.py` and constrained by
`analysis/test_integrity.py`.

### What is mechanically sound

- The fact is at order grain: **5,000 rows and 5,000 complete unique `order_id` values**.
- The calendar runs from **2023-01-01 through 2024-12-31**, covering all **731 dates** with no gap.
- All four fact foreign keys resolve, every dimension member is used, and all rider base-kitchen keys
  resolve. The archive is joinable.
- There are no exact duplicate rows or nulls in the delivered CSVs.

### What is not semantically sound

- **Delivery clocks do not measure the documented process.** Promised and actual time both span
  0-1,000 minutes. **4,892 of 5,000** promised values fall outside the documented 25-45 minute range,
  and **4,400** actual values exceed two hours.
- **The temperature field is not usable as Celsius.** **4,516 of 5,000** values lie above 100 Celsius;
  the median is 512.13 and the maximum is 999.91.
- **The rider speed field is not usable as km/h.** **56 of 60** rider averages exceed 100 km/h and the
  maximum is 992.4 km/h.
- **The profit label has no defensible accounting identity.** Every row differs from
  `order_value_ngn - delivery_cost_ngn` by more than NGN 0.01; the median absolute residual is
  NGN 70,368.33. `order_profit_ngn` contains **zero negative rows**, so three supplied profitability
  claims cannot be true under the delivered field.
- **The time dimension contradicts itself and the calendar.** All **48 of 48** `hour_of_day` values
  disagree with the start time in `slot_label`; **21 of 48** weekend flags disagree with weekday; and
  **4,288 of 5,000** orders have a calendar weekday different from their joined slot weekday.
- **Entity labels are unsafe grouping keys.** Five zone names each identify two different zone IDs;
  54 of 60 rider rows use a name shared with another rider ID.
- **Operational chronology breaks.** **1,238 orders** precede their kitchen's opening date. The fact
  kitchen differs from the rider's documented base kitchen on **3,736 orders**; the source does not
  state whether cross-kitchen assignment is permitted.
- **The supplied validator targets another schema.** Its report says **10 of 10 checks passed**, but
  **0 of 54** configured constraints names a delivered table-column pair and **3 of 7** configured
  relationships require absent fields. A fourth relationship connects `fact_orders.kitchen_id` to
  `dim_time_slot.time_slot_id`; both columns exist, but the relationship is semantically nonsensical.
- The blueprint's five dirty-data rules are empty null-injection templates with no target table or
  column, while the delivered CSVs contain no nulls. They do not explain the observed corruption.

## Top 5 data quality issues shaping this analysis

1. **Financial analysis is unsupported as labelled.** Profit is unrelated to the documented revenue
   and cost fields and cannot be negative, invalidating the archive's corridor-loss narrative.
2. **Service-time and food-quality analysis is unsupported as labelled.** Delivery minutes and
   temperature are dominated by physically or operationally implausible values.
3. **Temporal, weather and rush-hour analysis lacks a coherent clock.** Slot hour, weekday, weekend and
   order date conflict, so no source field reliably places an order in a real operating period.
4. **The documentation and validator describe a different release.** None of 54 configured constraints
   matches the delivered schema despite a 10/10 pass report.
5. **Clean joins conceal broken meaning.** Keys, row counts and calendar coverage pass, but the expected
   evidence chain from operating conditions to time, quality, rating and profit does not have valid
   measurements at each connector.
