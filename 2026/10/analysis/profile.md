## Conclusions

1. **The two facts are two different samples.** The daily fact is on the right scale (a mean day ×
   30.5 ≈ the monthly revenue) but does not reconcile: store-month r = 0.0307, store-level r = 0.2252.
   It is sparse (20.3% of store-days) and not unique at its stated grain. **Decision:** the monthly fact
   is the P&L of record; the daily fact is used only for within-day contrasts (weekday, bank holiday,
   staffing, mix) and never summed into money totals or joined to the P&L.
2. **Monthly revenue carries no store identity.** Store explains 4.1% of revenue variance, exactly the
   59/1,439 that 60 random labels would (Kruskal-Wallis p = 0.49). A store's 2023 mean revenue does not
   predict its 2024 mean (r = 0.0503). Rent (η² 0.98), staffing (0.86) and member share (0.92) are
   strongly store-persistent. **Whatever separates stores, it is not demand.**
3. **`profitability_status` cannot be used as delivered.** It disagrees with its own documented bands on
   600 rows, including 349 rows labelled `Profitable` with margins above 20%. **Decision:** status is
   re-derived from the margin in `build.py`; the supplied label is kept as `source_status` for audit.
4. **Format is not an analysable axis.** No Retail-Park store exists, and the Transport-Hub "format" is
   two train-station stores. Eleven High-Street stores are named "Station" or "Hub". The +6.62pp hub
   margin gap has permutation p = 0.2182 (20,000 draws).
5. **Rent and staffing in the P&L are monthly variants of the store contract**, not the contract itself
   (ratio to `dim_store` 0.84-1.16 and 0.72-1.24). **Decision:** the monthly values are used for P&L
   (they are what the identity C09 closes on); `dim_store` values are used for store attributes.
   `rent_band` is re-derived from monthly rent; 23 stores then change band month to month, so band is a
   store-month attribute, not a store attribute.

## Top 5 data quality issues shaping this analysis

1. The daily and monthly facts do not reconcile (C23): no daily total may be presented as P&L.
2. Store revenue is indistinguishable from noise across stores, regions and months: every revenue
   ranking is a ranking of noise and must say so.
3. `profitability_status` contradicts its own definition on 600 rows (C11): re-derive it.
4. Format has n = 2 against n = 58 and no Retail-Park (C01): replace the format axis where the brief
   allows, answer it with uncertainty where it does not.
5. The product catalogue cannot produce the P&L's gross margin (C20) and `product_id` is a top-product
   label: category margins describe the price list, not the business.
