## Top 5 data quality issues shaping this analysis

The five that changed what this analysis could say, in the order they bind:

1. **The 1,081 companies are a uniform random overlay on a real complaint register.** Every
   company-side association test lands on chance - 12 tests, χ²/df between **0.945 and 1.018** -
   while the 66 consumer-side tests run **1.17× to 15,629×** above it. There is no overlap. Any
   company league table, response-rate ranking or "worst performer" chart on this file ranks
   noise, and that is the month's thesis rather than a caveat to it.
2. **2,150 complaints were answered before they were received** (`date_responded <
   date_received`). Every identity the Data Dictionary states holds exactly, which is precisely
   why this was missed at first: **an identity check is not an integrity check.** Verifying that
   `a = f(b, c)` says nothing about whether `a`, `b` and `c` can coexist.
3. **1,494 complaints are still in progress and every one carries a response date.** Same shape
   as issue 2, and it makes any "unresolved" filter unreliable on the date columns.
4. **The file is right-censored, steeply.** In-progress runs 0.65% → 3.63% → 49.34% → 81.87%
   across the last four months. Dropping unresolved rows makes the newest period read almost
   perfect, because only the fast cases have resolved yet. **Censor, do not drop** - and note
   that excluding a partial period from a *warning* is not excluding it from a *fit*.
5. **`response_days` is fabricated: Uniform(0, 30), all 31 integer values present.** The
   precise-sounding clock is the invented one, while `date_received - date_submitted` sat
   unexamined and separates channels at ε² = 0.364. The most precise-sounding column is worth
   testing before it is trusted.

### DQ2 - Response time is a uniform draw, and "timely" does not measure speed

`Response_Time_Days` is **U(0,30)**: χ² p=0.071, mean 15.088 against 15.000 expected, sd 8.914
against 8.944. It is uniform **within** every channel, product, region and outcome as well -
0 of 19 subgroups reject (the June lesson).

And `Timely response?` is **independent of it**: "Yes" averaged 15.09 days, "No" averaged
15.05, Mann-Whitney **p=0.831**, both spanning the full 0-30 range. A timely response takes
exactly as long as an untimely one. **Brief Q5's "how fast" has no answer here.**

### DQ3 - Company timeliness is sampling noise

`Timely_Response_Rate` ranges 0.815-1.000 across 1,081 companies, sd **0.0320**. If every
company shared one true rate, the binomial expectation at n≈58 complaints each is **0.0318**.
Observed / expected = **1.005**. There is no real between-company variation to rank.

### DQ4 - The missingness is RIGHT-CENSORING, not structural, and it moves the headline

`Timely response?` is null on exactly the **1,494** in-progress complaints and nowhere else -
but that is not clean structural missingness. It is **right-censoring confined to the last four
months**: 2023-05 is 0.65% in progress, 2023-06 3.63%, **2023-07 49.34%, 2023-08 81.87%**.
Excluding the nulls makes the newest months read as near-perfect, because only the fast ones
have resolved. **Any outcome measure must censor from 2023-05 onward, not merely drop nulls.**

| Denominator | Timeliness |
|---|---:|
| counting In-progress as untimely | 93.77% |
| **excluding In-progress (correct)** | **96.06%** |

**Correction:** an earlier draft of `brief.md` published 93.77%. A complaint still in progress
has not failed to be timely - it has not yet been judged. Worth **2.30pp**.

`Sub-issue` is null on 10,858 rows (17.4%) and `Company public response` on 2,175 (3.5%);
neither is imputed.

### DQ5 - Two of the brief's ranking questions have no denominator

**Q2** asks which states are hotspots. The file has **no population or per-capita column**.
The top four states are CA, FL, TX, NY - 46.9% of all complaints, and the four most populous
states. Raw volume ranks states by size.

## Conclusions - what this file can and cannot support

**Cannot support:** any company-level comparison. Complaint volume is flat across size (DQ1),
timeliness variation is sampling noise (DQ3), response speed is a uniform draw (DQ2), and the
four company traits correlate with nothing - **0 of 12 pre-declared tests clear Bonferroni**,
against an MDE of 0.69-0.92pp on timeliness and ρ=0.085 on the numeric traits. The null is a
measurement, not a shrug. Nor can it support a state "hotspot" ranking (DQ5).

**Can support**, all on the consumer side:

1. **A real volume trend, on full months only.** **+8.70 complaints/month**, r=0.797. Volume
   grows **1.71×** from the first twelve full months to the last twelve - *not* "roughly
   triples", which an earlier draft of `brief.md` claimed while `integrity.py` printed 1.7×.
   **And 2023-07's apparent series maximum of 1,749 is not a volume record:** 863 of those are
   still in progress, so only **886 resolved** - below 2023-06's 981. I warned about the final
   month and then endorsed the more dangerous point one month earlier.
2. **Genuine product concentration.** The top two products - checking/savings and credit
   cards - are **65.6%** of every complaint; the top three are 77.9%.
3. **Genuine issue concentration.** 76 issues, top three = 39.1%.
4. **Outcome mix.** Closed with explanation 65.7%, monetary relief 23.5%, non-monetary 8.4%.
5. **Timeliness broke in 2021, and the 96.06% pooled figure hides it.** 99.83 / 99.85 / 99.90 /
   99.31 through 2020, then **89.02%** in 2021, 95.62% in 2022, and **89.52%** in 2023 once the
   right-censored months are held out (93.16% if they are pooled - those four months resolve at
   96.4 / 99.5 / 100 / 100% because only the fast cases have closed). A regime break, not a
   drift, and **not compositional**: 15 of the 16 product, channel and region cuts with n≥30
   either side of the boundary fall by more than 5pp. **Mortgage never breaks** (99.58% in
   2021), the one exception.
6. **Relief concentrates, and that is the supervision priority list.** Monetary relief rate
   across 29 issues with n≥300 runs **0.86% to 50.77%** - a 59× spread, Cramér **V=0.346**
   (χ²=7,150) - an order of magnitude larger than anything on the company side. **Five
   product-issue pairs carry 65.4% of all monetary relief on 42.2% of complaints.**
7. **The intake lag is the only real speed measure**, and Q5 was declared unanswerable while
   looking at the fabricated one. `Date received - Date submitted` separates channels
   enormously - Referral delayed on **75.6%** of complaints against Web's **7.8%**, Kruskal
   ε²=0.364 - and does **not** vary by company (p=0.347), confirming the company thesis on a
   column it had never been tested on.
