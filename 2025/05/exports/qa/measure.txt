--- appendix: poster CLIP check (2025-05-27) ---
measure.mjs measures the poster ROOT, which is `overflow: hidden` at a fixed 2560x1440.
It therefore reports FITS even when .grid-main has been squeezed and is clipping a table
row. That blind spot produced a real regression during this remediation: longer So-what
copy shrank .grid-main and silently cut the last market row and its thin-sample footnote
off the poster. The check below is the one that catches it, run here for the record.

.grid-main height 901px, content needs 901px  -> slack 0px
.grid-main bottom edge 1108px
  data table: 4 body rows, bottom 1093px (INSIDE)
  data table: 4 body rows, bottom 1060px (INSIDE)

RESULT: no table is clipped. Both the price-band legend (4 rows) and the market
table (4 rows + thin-sample footnote) render whole on the poster.
