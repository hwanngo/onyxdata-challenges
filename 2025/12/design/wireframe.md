## Grid

Four named areas, four items: `head · signature · truth · action`. Counted and asserted -
2025/09 lost a panel below the fold to an unassigned child, and 2025/11 shipped a poster
clipped to 1440px because the shell constrained the sheet. Both are now dna-kit's problem.

## Encoding rules

- **as filed** → hollow mark `○`, dashed stroke, amber
- **as measured** → solid mark `●`, solid stroke, navy
- **the measure that holds** → green, used exactly once on the page
- Fill carries meaning; hue reinforces. Verified under three CVD simulations and greyscale.
- Every figure Roboto Mono, tabular, so the two readings align digit-for-digit.
